Aarne Talman

I study whether language models understand language, and how we could tell.

About

I am a researcher in natural language processing and a Visiting Scholar in language technology at the University of Helsinki, where I also teach the course Large Language Models and Generative AI for NLP. Alongside research, I work as Senior Manager, Software Development at AMD Silo AI.

I received my PhD in language technology from the University of Helsinki in 2024. Before that I studied computational linguistics and formal grammar at King’s College London and philosophy at the London School of Economics.

Research

Natural language understanding
Whether models that score well on inference benchmarks capture meaning, or exploit shortcuts in the data. My doctoral work showed that models can keep much of their performance even when the data is deliberately corrupted, and that results often fail to carry over from one benchmark to another.
Evaluating generative language models
I co-founded ELOQUENT, a lab of shared tasks at CLEF for assessing the quality of generative language models, and organise tasks within it.
Formal grammar and semantics
Grammar formalisms, formal semantics and what they can tell us about the abilities and limits of neural language models.
